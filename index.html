  • Continuous optimization for enterprise-scale log volume

  • Phase 2 — Implementation: SIEM deployment, log source onboarding, initial detection rules & SOAR workflow build-out
  • Phase 3 — Tuning & Handover: Alert tuning against live traffic, knowledge transfer, formal go-live sign-off
  • Phase 4 — Post-Launch Support (6 months included): Rule & workflow tuning, troubleshooting, ad-hoc incident support, minor scope adjustments
